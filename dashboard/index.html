Scams Prevention Framework: what auto-reimbursement will cost banks, and where to act first

From 31 March 2027 Australian banks become legally responsible for preventing and responding to scams. Treasury also proposes that verified scam losses under $3,000 be refunded automatically. This page sizes that cost, checks whether banks' scam data can support it, and ranks the controls that reduce the losses refunds do not cover.

5. Recommendations

  1. Adopt one scam taxonomy and data model (scam type, channel, payment rail, loss, recovery, refund) across fraud operations, IDR and SPF reporting, by February 2027. Owner: Data & Analytics.
  2. Build a straight-through "verify and refund" path for claims under $3,000 and keep full investigation for larger claims, by March 2027. Owner: Fraud & Scams Operations with Complaints.
  3. Provision at P50 and stress-test at P90, then re-run the model every quarter with real claims. The biggest unknowns are growth, the median loss, the verification rate and extra claims. Owner: Finance.
  4. Pilot holds on first-time high-risk payments with a randomised hold-out (about 14,600 flagged payments per arm to detect a 30% fall at a 1% baseline), then roll out Confirmation of Payee. Owner: Payments product.
