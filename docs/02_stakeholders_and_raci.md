# 2. Stakeholders and RACI

## Stakeholder map
| Stakeholder | Interest in the SPF change | Influence | What they need from this analysis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chief Risk Officer / SPF accountable executive | Compliance by 31 Mar 2027; $50m penalty exposure | High | Cost range, readiness gaps, decision on priorities |
| Head of Fraud & Scams Operations | Claim volumes, staffing, investigation workload | High | Eligible-claim volumes, hours saved, verification rules |
| Customer Advocate / Complaints (IDR) | RG 271 timeframes, AFCA escalations | Medium | Escalation benchmark, process redesign |
| Finance (provisioning) | Budget for reimbursements from FY2027 | High | P50 / P90 cost for provisioning and stress testing |
| Digital Payments product owner | Customer friction from holds and Confirmation of Payee | Medium | Expected avoided loss vs friction, pilot design |
| Data & Analytics | Taxonomy, data model, SPF reporting | Medium | Data requirements and data-quality findings |
| Regulators (ASIC, ACCC, AFCA) | Consistent, reliable reporting and fair outcomes | High (external) | Accurate SPF reports, timely resolution |
| Customers, especially aged 65+ | Fast refunds, fewer scams | High (external) | Simple claim path, effective warnings |

## RACI for the recommended programme
R = Responsible, A = Accountable, C = Consulted, I = Informed

| Workstream | CRO / SPF exec | Fraud & Scams Ops | Complaints (IDR) | Finance | Payments product | Data & Analytics |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| W1 Common scam taxonomy & data model | A | C | C | I | I | R |
| W2 Straight-through "verify & refund" path for claims < $3,000 | A | R | R | C | C | C |
| W3 Provisioning for auto-reimbursement (P50 / P90) | C | C | I | A/R | I | C |
| W4 High-risk payment holds & limits (pilot then scale) | A | C | I | I | R | R (pilot analysis) |
| W5 Confirmation of Payee rollout | A | C | I | I | R | C |
| W6 SPF KPI dashboard & regulator reporting | A | C | C | I | I | R |
